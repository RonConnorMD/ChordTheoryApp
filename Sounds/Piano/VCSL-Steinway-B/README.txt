VCSL Steinway B samples used for Learn Chords playback

These AAC samples are the medium velocity layer (v2) from the compressed
steinway-b sample set. The set is derived from the NoSus close-mic recordings
of the Steinway B in the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL).

Original library: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
VCSL license: CC0 1.0
Compressed sample source: https://github.com/StanislavMakhnytskyi/stapiano-samples

The app uses the twelve whole-tone samples from MIDI 60 through MIDI 82
(C4 through A#5) and pitch-shifts by at most one semitone for intervening
notes and B5. The samples are mono AAC files at 44.1 kHz.
